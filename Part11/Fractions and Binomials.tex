The binomial coefficient, \(\binom{n}{k}\), is defined by the expression:
\[
\binom{n}{k} = \frac{n!}{k!(n-k)!}
\]
Fractions can be used inline within the paragraph text, for 
example \(\frac{1}{2}\), or displayed on their own line, 
such as this:
\[\frac{1}{2}\]
