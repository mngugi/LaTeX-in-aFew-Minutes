	Lists are easily to create:
	
	\begin{itemsize}
		
		\item List entries start with the \verb|\item| command.
		\item Individual entries are indicated with a black dot, a so-called bullet.
		\item The text in the entries may be of any length.
		
		\end{itemsize}
